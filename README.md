# CEO Desk

> **One calm place to capture your day, tasks, and reflections across Company, Self, and Life.**

An offline-first, private personal executive operating system built with React Native (Expo), TypeScript, and local SQLite. Designed with an ultra-clean, minimal aesthetic featuring an Obsidian & Brushed Champagne Gold palette.

---

## ✨ Features

- **🎯 Today (Command Center):**
  - Top 3 daily non-negotiable priorities
  - Today's prioritized task stream
  - Fast morning check-in (energy & sleep) & evening debrief (mood, workout, 1-line win)
  - One-tap quick capture for tasks and notes

- **📋 Tasks (Execution Without Clutter):**
  - Company & Personal tags
  - Priority matrix: High, Medium, Low
  - Filter by *Today*, *Upcoming*, and *Done*
  - Fast inline entry with zero friction

- **📝 Notes (Executive Knowledge):**
  - Tags (`Ideas`, `Meetings`, `Personal`, `Company`, `Strategy`, and custom tags)
  - Live instant fuzzy search
  - Pin important notes
  - Interactive checklists nested inside notes

- **📖 Journal (Daily Reflection):**
  - 4-Quadrant logging: *Mental*, *Physical*, *Emotional*, and *Company*
  - Executive review prompts: *"What I did"*, *"What I learned"*, *"What's next"*
  - Calendar scrubber to revisit past days

- **📊 Insights (Weekly Momentum):**
  - Mood & energy trends
  - Workout streaks & task completion analytics
  - Sunday Executive Review mode

- **🔒 Privacy & Offline First:**
  - 100% stored locally on your device with SQLite
  - Optional biometric (Fingerprint / FaceID) or PIN app lock
  - One-tap JSON backup export and import

---

## 🎨 Design Philosophy
- **Colors:** Deep Obsidian (`#0D0E11` / `#16181F`), Brushed Champagne Gold (`#C5A880`), Nordic Paper (`#F8F8F6`).
- **Typography:** Refined, spacious executive typography with high signal-to-noise ratio.
- **Interactions:** Subtle haptic feedback and gentle micro-animations.
